Task 02

Sycophancy Detection

Detecting sycophantic behavior in LLMs — the tendency to echo user beliefs regardless of truth

Coming Soon

Task 2 will challenge participants to build systems that detect sycophantic behavior in large language models. Sycophancy occurs when a model agrees with a user's stated opinion or belief, even when that opinion is factually incorrect or contradicted by evidence.

Participants will work with prompts designed to elicit sycophantic responses, and will develop methods to classify whether a given LLM output exhibits sycophantic tendencies. Details on data format, evaluation metrics, and baseline systems will be shared upon data release.
